Strategic judgment
Grok Imagine is a strong primary generator right now for the stated use case (short, realistic Business Spotlight-style clips with dialogue/music/ambience). Its development trajectory (Elon’s explicit full-feature ambition) makes it a smart long-term bet relative to paying for multiple overlapping short-form tools. It is not yet a complete video-production system; post-generation assembly, branding, captions, and multi-clip continuity remain necessary.
K3 is currently the strongest candidate for the operational-intelligence / batch-research / browser-automation role on the basis of its advertised combination of 1 M-token context, Swarm, Claw, etc. It has not yet been proven in the user’s actual workflow, and credit economics may force a hybrid pattern (K3 for hard parallel jobs, cheaper models for routine work).
The five-prospect test outlined in the conversation is exactly the right next step. It will surface the real numbers on research accuracy, credit burn, browser reliability, media usability, human effort, and end-to-end cost—data that no amount of documentation review can replace.
Bottom line: The conversation demonstrates healthy adversarial collaboration between models. Treat the refined stack and the live-test plan as the working hypothesis. Do not treat any model’s “best” claim as settled until the test results are in.
