CLEAR EXPECTATIONS

Before you
take the next step.

Will this guarantee AI recommendations?

No. GIG cannot guarantee rankings, inclusion, citations, traffic, leads or revenue. AI answers vary by system, location, retrieval mode, model, personalization and time. Observed changes do not establish causation.

What if the numbers do not improve?

The report still documents the observation cycle, the verified asset, the bounded improvement, and unresolved issues. A flat result is reported as a flat result. Work completed and external movement are shown separately.

Do you need access to my accounts?

The core service works on GIG-controlled assets. It does not require your Facebook, Google Business Profile or website passwords. Problems on client-owned properties are flagged; account management, manual photo uploads, review responses, website maintenance and ongoing social posting are excluded.

Which AI systems are included?

The approved observation panel determines coverage for the pilot. The names above describe the customer question; they are not a guaranteed platform bundle or an affiliation. Provider access and evidence rights must be validated before any live service begins.

What happens when a source is unavailable?

Missing data is marked unavailable. Prior evidence is retained with its original context and is not presented as a new result or a zero. A material factual conflict pauses publication until it is resolved.

Can I sign up or pay here?

This is an internal staging preview. Enrollment, payment, form submission and live service delivery are unavailable. Cancellation, refund, retention and final onboarding terms must be disclosed and approved before activation.
